# mp_counter

This script is used in conjunction with a experimental method designed by DCCEEW NSW only.
Microplastics caught in a net trawling method are cleaned and dyed with Nile Red.
The Nile Red attaches to the micrplastics and glows orange under blue light.
The microplastics are presented in a petri dish under a camera with blue light.
The camera images look like orange spots over a black ground.

The script counts these oranges spots and tallys them.

Script requires naming the filenames of images with suffixes of either "_SML", "_MED", or "_LRG" to tag as small, medium or large.
